> Here, feedback and personal opinions about the experience of participating in the First Cloud AI Journey program can be shared freely to help the FCAJ team improve any shortcomings based on the following aspects:

### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment is very friendly and open. FCAJ members are always willing to help whenever difficulties arise, even outside working hours. The workspace is tidy and comfortable, helping to focus better. However, it is suggested to add more social gatherings or team bonding activities to strengthen relationships.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
The mentor provides very detailed guidance, explains clearly when concepts are unclear, and always encourages asking questions. The admin team supports administrative tasks, provides necessary documents, and creates favorable conditions to work effectively. It is especially appreciated that the mentor allows trying to solve problems independently instead of just giving the answer.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
The assigned tasks align well with the knowledge learned at university, while also introducing new areas that were not previously encountered. This served to both strengthen foundational knowledge and gain practical skills.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
During the internship, many new skills were learned, such as using project management tools, teamwork, and professional communication in a corporate environment. The mentor also shared valuable real-world experiences that helped plan a career path.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
The company culture is very positive: everyone respects each other, works seriously but still keeps things enjoyable. When there are urgent projects, everyone works together and supports one another regardless of position. This created a strong sense of belonging, even at the internship level.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
The company offers flexible time arrangements when necessary. Additionally, being granted the opportunity to attend internal training sessions is a significant advantage.

---

### Additional Questions

* **What was most satisfying during the internship?**  
  It was highly satisfying to have direct access to real AWS Cloud environments, design and configure the infrastructure for the individual project, and receive dedicated mentorship from experienced AWS Vietnam engineers.

* **What should the company improve for future cohorts?**  
  As the bootcamp is fast-paced and intensive, it is suggested that the organizers allocate more time for guided hands-on labs covering complex services like advanced VPC networking, deep IAM configurations, and AWS-native CI/CD deployment pipelines.

* **Would this program be recommended to others? Why or why not?**  
  Yes, definitely. This is one of the highest-quality technology internship programs, providing an ideal real-world playground that combines Cloud Computing with Generative AI (using Bedrock and Agents).

---

### Suggestions & Expectations

* **Suggestions to improve the internship experience:**  
  Organizing more offline networking events or career pathway sharing sessions between interns and official AWS engineers.

* **Is there an interest in continuing this program in the future?**  
  There is high interest in continuing to support the First Cloud AI Journey community, potentially by sharing experiences or assisting future cohorts.

* **Any other comments (free sharing):**  
  Sincere gratitude is expressed to the mentors and administration of the First Cloud AI Journey program for their guidance and support throughout the internship at AWS Vietnam.
