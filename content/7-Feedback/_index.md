### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment is very friendly and open. FCAJ members are always willing to help whenever difficulties arise, even outside working hours.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
The mentor provides very detailed guidance, explains clearly when there is content that is not understood, and always encourages asking questions. The admin team supports procedures and documentation and creates favorable working conditions. It is highly appreciated that the mentor allows independent testing and problem solving instead of just giving answers.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
The assigned tasks align well with the knowledge learned at school, while also expanding into new areas that were not previously encountered. This serves to both consolidate foundational knowledge and acquire practical skills.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
During the internship, many new skills were learned, such as using project management tools, teamwork, and professional communication in the corporate environment. The mentor also shared much practical experience to help better direct the career path.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
The company culture is very positive: everyone respects each other, working seriously but still keeping it enjoyable. When there are urgent projects, everyone strives together and supports one another regardless of position. This creates a sense of belonging to the team, even at the internship level.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
The company provides an internship allowance and offers flexible working hours when necessary. In addition, having the opportunity to join internal training sessions is a big plus.


### Additional Questions

* **What was most satisfying during the internship?**  
  Highly satisfied with having direct access to real AWS Cloud environments, designing and configuring the infrastructure for the individual project, and receiving dedicated guidance from experts (Mentors) at AWS Vietnam.

* **Would this program be recommended to others? Why or why not?**  
  Yes, definitely. This is one of the highest quality technology internship programs, providing an ideal practical environment.
